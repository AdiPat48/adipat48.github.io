## pjc_about
The **Protein Journal Club (PJC)** at NCBS is a student-run group dedicated to discussing exciting and thought-provoking papers at the intersection of protein science, structural biology, and computational methods.

With my colleagues Aysha and Harika, I organised the PJC from 2023 to 2025, managing all aspects of coordination: speaker scheduling, outreach, participant recruitment, venue and logistics, and refreshments. I also designed the journal club's visual identity, including promotional posters and the PJC logo.
