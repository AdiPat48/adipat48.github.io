## intro
In my research, I focus on the structural analysis of biological systems, the construction of specialized databases, and the development of predictive computational tools.

## project_1
### Decoding the Structural Logic of Gap Junction Design
category: Biological Application
image: assets/images/website_images_gapj.svg
description: Gap junctions are multimeric channels that enable direct cell–cell communication, yet arise from evolutionarily unrelated protein families. This raises a fundamental question: why do divergent sequences converge on a similar monomeric blueprint to form functional channels? Using integrative structural bioinformatics and residue-level energetic profiling, we decode the universal design principles that constrain this shared architecture and its functional properties. In parallel, we are computationally modelling gap junctions to investigate non-canonical oligomerization states and stoichiometric diversity.
keywords: Structure-guided sequence alignment · hotspot prediction · evolutionary analysis · protein blocks · entropy calculations
ref: Pathak A, Sowdhamini R. Computational structural analysis sheds light on the molecular grammar of gap junction proteins. Biochimie. 2025.

## project_2
### Protein-Glycan Interactions in Plant Lectins
category: Biological Application
image: assets/images/website_images_cus17.svg
description: Cus17 is a phloem lectin from *Cucumis sativus* that mediates plant defense by recognizing carbohydrate signatures on insect and fungal surfaces, yet its interactions with higher oligosaccharides remain structurally unresolved. We combined molecular docking, binding-site prediction, and molecular dynamics simulations to map ligand binding across canonical and non-canonical sites, and to explore multivalent interactions within dimeric and modelled tetrameric assemblies. This work reveals a complex binding landscape, suggesting that ligand specificity, alternative binding sites, and oligomerization collectively shape the recognition of phloem invaders by plant lectins.
keywords: Ligand docking · Molecular dynamics simulations · MM-GBSA binding free energy calculations · Unbinding event analysis
ref: Tiwari V*, Pathak A*, Poojary T, Sowdhamini R, Surolia A. Exploration of structural space of Cus17 lectin with glycans using molecular docking and simulation studies. Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal. 2025.

category: Database
image: assets/images/website_images_grayu.svg

## project_4
### PIMADb 2.0: An Updated Database of Protein–Protein Interactions in Macromolecular Assemblies
category: Database
image: assets/images/website_images_pimadb2.svg
description: PIMADb 2.0 builds on the earlier version of the database of protein–protein interactions in macromolecular assemblies, which uses the in-house PPCheck framework to quantify inter-chain interactions via pseudoenergies. The update expands coverage across the Protein Data Bank and adds features such as predicted hotspots, solvent accessibility, symmetry, and interface clustering. Currently under development, it will enable researchers to explore and compare protein interfaces at both global and residue-level detail.

## project_5
### Computational Prediction of Protein–Protein Interaction Hotspots
category: Tool
image: assets/images/website_images_hotspot_prediction.svg
description: A small subset of residues at protein–protein interfaces, termed hotspots, dominate binding free energy and define the stability of macromolecular complexes. Identifying these residues is central to understanding interaction energetics, yet remains challenging to achieve experimentally at scale. Computational approaches — from early physics-based methods to recent machine learning models — have therefore emerged as powerful alternatives. We have extensively reviewed these approaches and proposed that integrating molecular dynamics with machine learning can enable faster and more comprehensive hotspot identification. In parallel, we developed [**DeepHotspot-Seq**](https://github.com/AdiPat48/DL-HSPred), a sequence-only deep learning framework that leverages protein language model embeddings to predict hotspots in proteins lacking structural information.
keywords: Hotspot Prediction · Protein Language Models · Deep Learning · Classical Machine Learning · Sequence-to-function Prediction
ref: [Book Chapter] Pathak A, Tiwari V, Menon R, Sowdhamini R. Prediction of Protein–Protein Interactions. Encyclopedia of Bioinformatics and Computational Biology (Second Edition). 2025.
ref: [Review] Pathak A, Tiwari V, and Sowdhamini R. Emerging strategies for computational identification of protein–protein interaction hotspots. Current Opinion in Structural Biology. Accepted.
