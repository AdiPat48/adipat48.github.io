## intro
Before registering for my PhD, I completed three rotation projects and a summer internship that shaped my research interests and technical repertoire.

## rotation_3
### Rotation 3 · Integrative Structural Biology Lab, NCBS (Feb–Mar 2022)
**Supervisor:** Dr. Shruthi Viswanath

I established performance benchmarks to validate <a href="https://github.com/isblab/nestor" style="font-weight: bold; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NestOR<span style="font-size: 0.85em; margin-left: 2px; display: inline-block; text-decoration: none;">↗</span></a>, a Bayesian framework for optimizing structural representations. Using the <a href="https://integrativemodeling.org/" style="font-weight: bold; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Integrative Modeling Platform (IMP)<span style="font-size: 0.85em; margin-left: 2px; display: inline-block; text-decoration: none;">↗</span></a>, I generated structural ensembles for RNA Pol II and the γ-tubulin small complex (gTUSC) across resolutions from 1 to 100 residues per bead. I quantified trade-offs between CPU sampling time, ensemble precision (RMSD), and agreement with cross-linking and EM data.

**Publication:** Arvindekar S, Pathak AS, Majila K, Viswanath S#. Optimizing representations for integrative structural modeling using Bayesian model selection. *Bioinformatics*. 2024.

## rotation_1
### Rotation 1 · Organelle Biology Lab, NCBS (Oct–Nov 2021)
**Supervisor:** Dr. Swadhin Chandra Jana

I worked on a preliminary RNAi screen for ciliary proteins in Drosophila. I learned fly pushing techniques and set up crosses to generate knockdowns using the Gal4–UAS system. I performed phenotype screening through eclosion and pupal development monitoring, along with negative geotaxis (climbing) assays to infer sensory and cilia-associated functional defects.

## internship_1
### Summer Internship · SVKM's C.B. Patel Research Centre, Mumbai (June 2019)
**Supervisor:** Dr. Niyati Mudliar

I used fluorimetric and spectrophotometric assays with dye-based biosensors to detect and quantify heparin, and to monitor insulin fibril formation. I analysed the resulting spectral datasets to assess the sensitivity and dynamic range of the detection.
