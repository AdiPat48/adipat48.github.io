## intro
Before registering for my PhD, I completed three rotation projects and a summer internship that shaped my research interests and technical repertoire.

## rotation_1
### Rotation 1 · Integrative Structural Biology Lab, NCBS (Feb–Mar 2022)
**Supervisor:** Dr. Shruthi Viswanath

I established performance benchmarks to validate NestOR (Arvindekar, Pathak et al., 2024), a Bayesian framework for optimizing structural representations. Using the Integrative Modeling Platform (IMP), I generated structural ensembles for RNA Pol II and the γ-tubulin small complex (gTUSC) across resolutions from 1 to 100 residues per bead. I quantified trade-offs between CPU sampling time, ensemble precision (RMSD), and agreement with cross-linking and EM data — providing foundational metrics for multi-scale model selection.

**Publication:** Arvindekar S, Pathak AS, Majila K, Viswanath S. Optimizing representations for integrative structural modeling using Bayesian model selection. *Bioinformatics*. 2024.

## rotation_2
### Rotation 2 · Organelle Biology Lab, NCBS (Oct–Nov 2021)
**Supervisor:** Dr. Swadhin Chandra Jana

I worked on a preliminary RNAi screen for ciliary proteins in Drosophila. I learned fly pushing techniques and set up crosses to generate knockdowns using the Gal4–UAS system. I performed phenotype screening through eclosion and pupal development monitoring, along with negative geotaxis (climbing) assays to infer sensory and cilia-associated functional defects.

## internship_1
### Summer Internship · SVKM's C.B. Patel Research Centre, Mumbai (June 2019)
**Supervisor:** Dr. Niyati Mudliar

I performed fluorimetric and spectrophotometric assays to quantify Heparin and Insulin fibril concentrations using biosensor dyes, analyzing spectral datasets to evaluate molecular detection and aggregation of bio-analytes.
